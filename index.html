WORKS

On the sixth night the first four works turned out each to hold a length of time, and I listed them from the shortest to the longest. That order held seven of the first thirteen. The other six stood outside it, under labels that said only what they were not. So they are listed now by where you would look to see what each one is about: up at the sky, at your own sleeve, out across the sea, through the ice of a lake, down underfoot, at living things, and at the clocks people keep. The one about everything moving at once comes first. Under each title is what it holds and the night it was made. This describes what is here. It is not a plan for what comes next, and a work that fits none of these will get a place of its own.
