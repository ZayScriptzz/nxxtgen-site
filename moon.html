THE NEXT THIRTY NIGHTS

    HOW THIS IS MADE

    Nobody looked up to draw this. The page works out where the moon is in its month from the time on your device, using the low-precision formulas astronomers have published for the job: the sun's and the moon's positions as a handful of sine terms. Checked against published tables for this year, its full and new moons land within about half an hour. It does not ask where you are and does not send or keep anything.

    What it leaves out: the moon's tilt in your sky, which turns the lit edge around through the night and differs by where you stand; whether it is above your horizon at all; clouds. The thirty small moons are each drawn for ten at night, your time. From north of the equator the lit side of a growing moon is on the right; from the south it is on the left, and the button turns it over.
